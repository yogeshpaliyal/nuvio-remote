Found something to watch? Share the link to Skiff and it opens on your TV, in the right app. A remote too, with your phone's keyboard. No developer options needed.

Three taps, couch to screen.

The first time, pair with the code your TV shows, like the Google TV app. After that it's just share.

Share

From YouTube, JioHotstar, IMDb or any app: Share → Skiff.

Pick your TV

Skiff finds the TVs on your Wi-Fi by name. No address to type.

Play

It opens on the TV, in the app that plays it. IMDb titles open in Nuvio.

Small app. Does the job.

Everything happens between your phone and your TV, on your Wi-Fi.

No developer options

Pair once with a code on the TV. USB debugging is optional, for extras.

A real remote

D-pad, volume, playback, power. Your phone's volume buttons work too.

Type on the TV

Search with your phone's keyboard instead of the on-screen one.

The right app

Links open in the TV's own app for them, with rules that improve without updates.

History & Saved

Everything you sent, and a list for later. Kept on your phone.

Quick Settings tile

The remote, one swipe away from anywhere on your phone.

No account. No ads.

History stays on your phone. Skiff sends crash reports and anonymous usage, never what you watch, and one switch turns that off.
